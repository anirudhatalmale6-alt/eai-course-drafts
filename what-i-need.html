Before you send anything: de-identifying

Please do not send me anything with a real client identifiable in it. Not because of the chat, but because it is your obligation and it is not worth the risk for a training example.

Strip these before sending, and check the file rather than trusting yourself to remember:

  • Client and entity names, ABNs, ACNs and TFNs
  • People's names, including staff who prepared or reviewed
  • Addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, bank and account numbers
  • Anything in a header, a footer or a filename, which is where these survive
  • In Excel, the document properties and any hidden sheets or comments

Shift dates and round the numbers if you like. It changes nothing for teaching purposes. What must stay real is the shape of the thing: the messy exception, the awkward clause, the coding rule that catches people. That is the only part I cannot make up.

If an item cannot be safely de-identified, skip it and tell me. I will write the generic version and mark it, and we lose a little authority on that one lesson rather than creating a problem for you.
