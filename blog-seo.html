3. Write for AI search (GEO), the reusable structure

AI search engines quote content that answers a question directly and is easy to lift. For your top posts, and everything new, use this shape. It also helps human readers.

  1. Answer first. Open with 2 to 3 sentences that directly answer the title's question, before any preamble. This is the part AI tools quote.
  2. Question-style headings. Use H2s a reader would actually type or ask, for example "How does AI monitor ATO changes?" rather than "Monitoring".
  3. Short FAQ at the end. 3 to 5 real questions with 2 to 3 sentence answers. This is prime material for AI answers and Google's People Also Ask.
  4. Key takeaways box. A short bulleted summary near the top or bottom.
  5. Link out to your own pages. Link to Luna, the Academy and related posts, so a reader (and a crawler) can go deeper.

4. Author and trust signals (E-E-A-T)

For finance topics, Google and AI engines weigh who wrote it very heavily. Right now posts are bylined only "Empathetic AI".

  • Set a real author display name in Users → Profile (a named person, ideally a finance-credentialled team member), with a 2 to 3 line bio ending in the credential.
  • Make sure there is a strong About page: who you are, the OpenAI Select Partner status, and the team's finance background. Link to it from the site menu.
  • Add a one-line author bio at the foot of each post. I can draft these once you tell me the author name and credentials.

5. Small but worthwhile

  • Featured image on every post. WordPress uses it as the social preview (og:image). A couple of posts are missing one.
  • Image alt text. Add a short, descriptive alt to each image (helps accessibility and image search).
  • Categories and tags. Keep them tight and consistent (AI for Finance, Governance, Australian Tax, Agentic AI). Avoid one-off tags.
  • Internal links. From each post, link to 2 or 3 related posts and to the Academy once it launches. This is one of the easiest ranking lifts.
  • Submit the sitemap (blog.empathetic-ai.com/sitemap.xml) in Google Search Console if not already, so new posts get found faster.
